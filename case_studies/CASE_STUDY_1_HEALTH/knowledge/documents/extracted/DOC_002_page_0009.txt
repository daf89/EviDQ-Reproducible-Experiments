Screening programs are available in 
Europe (e.g., Fr1da and gppad.org), Aus

Prediabetes
Prediabetes is the term used to identify 
glucose or A1C levels that do not meet 
the criteria for diabetes yet fall in an 
intermediate range between normo
glycemia and diabetes (25,83). People 
with prediabetes are defined by the 
presence of IFG and/or IGT and/or A1C 
5.7–6.4% (39–47 mmol/mol) (Table 2.2).

progression to diabetes as well as cardio
