SECTION I: USE OF THE CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDELINE
This Clinical Practice Guideline document is based upon literature searches conducted from July 2022 through April 2023 and
updated in July 2023. It is designed to assist decision-making. It is not intended to deﬁne a standard of care and should not be
interpreted as prescribing an exclusive course of management. Variations in practice will inevitably and appropriately occur
when clinicians consider the needs of individual patients, available resources, and limitations unique to an institution or type of
practice. Healthcare providers using the statements in this document (both practice points and recommendations) should
decide how to apply them to their own clinical practice.
