Values and preferences. The Work Group judged that most
well-informed people with CKD would prefer to optimize
medical therapies that have proven beneﬁt for CKD pro-

treatment of asymptomatic hyperuricemia to modify the risk
of CKD progression.

uric acid–lowering agents.
Rationale
There is insufﬁcient evidence to recommend the use of uric
acid–lowering therapies in asymptomatic hyperuricemia for
the speciﬁc purpose of delaying CKD progression. We make
the recommendation not giving uric acid–lowering therapy in
asymptomatic hyperuricemia for slowing of kidney disease
based on the current evidence that suggests unclear beneﬁts.
We judge that it is best practice not to expose people to
medications that provide little beneﬁt.
