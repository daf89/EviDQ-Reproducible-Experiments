Box 2:	Advantages and disadvantages of CCBs
Advantages:
••
Probably effective for all races;
••
Reduces need for monitoring of electrolytes and renal function;
••
Avoids need for different treatment for women of childbearing age who may become 
pregnant.
Disadvantages:
••
May be more expensive than diuretics;
••
Ankle edema may occur in up to 10% of patients, particularly with intensification 
dose in the absence of an ACE-inhibitor or ARB.
