Box 3:	Advantages and disadvantages of ACE inhibitors
Advantages:
••
Benefits some patients with kidney disease, prior myocardial infarction, and low 
ejection fraction.
Disadvantages:
••
May be more expensive than other hypertension medications;
••
A persistent cough is experienced by up to 10% of patients treated with an ACE 
inhibitor (not observed with ARBs); this risk is higher in people of African descent;
••
Less effective as single medication in people of African descent;
••
Small risk of angioedema; risk is higher in people of African descent (not observed 
with ARBs);
••
ACE inhibitors (and ARBs) should NOT be given to women who are or who may 
become pregnant;
••
Risk of hyperkalemia, especially if patient has CKD. 
Note: Research suggests that ARBs may have effectiveness similar to ACE inhibitors.
