Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
36. 	Diabetes prevalence
Indicator name
Diabetes prevalence
Purpose
To measure outcome of diabetes prevention interventions.
Definition
Proportion of people aged 18 years and over with raised blood glucose or on 
medication for diabetes. 
Raised blood glucose criteria:
	
●
FPG ≥ 126 mg/dl (7 mmol/L); or
	
●
HbA1c ≥ 6.5% (48 mmol/mol).
Numerator
Number of respondents aged 18 years and over with raised blood glucose or on 
medication for diabetes. 
Denominator
Total number of respondents aged 18 years and over.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, individual characteristics such as age, sex, race/
ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence 
type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Nationally or sub-nationally representative population-based surveys, with 
blood glucose measurements and a questionnaire, which investigates receiving 
medicines for diabetes.
Limitations/comments
For any comparison over time with other populations, age 
standardization is needed.
Related links
