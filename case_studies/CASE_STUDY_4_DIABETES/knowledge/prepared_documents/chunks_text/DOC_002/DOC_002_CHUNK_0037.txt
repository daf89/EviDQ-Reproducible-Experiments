37. 	Hospitalization for diabetes
Indicator name
Hospitalization for diabetes 
Purpose
To assess outcome of diabetes management.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who were admitted to hospital due to 
following conditions:
1.	 Severe hypoglycaemia 
2.	 Diabetic ketoacidosis
3.	 Hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state
4.	 Myocardial infarction
5.	 Stroke
6.	 Lower-extremity amputation
7.	 Kidney failure
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in the facility who were admitted to 
hospital due to diabetes-related complications including cardiovascular disease 
during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in the facility.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by cause of admission, facility level 
of care type, provider ownership type (public/private), and individual-level 
characteristics such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-
risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence type (urban/rural), and health 
insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
For comparison with other health care facilities the indicator needs to be 
age-standardized.

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
38. 	Cardiovascular disease among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Cardiovascular disease among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess outcome of diabetes management.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who were diagnosed with cardiovascular 
disease based on WHO or national guidelines.
Cardiovascular disease among people with diabetes, includes:
	
●
myocardial infarction;
	
●
stroke; and
	
●
lower-extremity vascular occlusion.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in the facility who were diagnosed 
with cardiovascular disease during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in the facility.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
For comparison with other health care facilities the indicator needs to be 
age-standardized.
Related links
HEARTS-D: diagnosis and management of type 2 diabetes. Geneva: World Health 
Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/331710). ICD 11 mortality 
and morbidity statistics: GB61 Chronic kidney disease [website]. Geneva: 
World Health Organization; 2024 (https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/
en#412389819, accessed 26 May 2024).
