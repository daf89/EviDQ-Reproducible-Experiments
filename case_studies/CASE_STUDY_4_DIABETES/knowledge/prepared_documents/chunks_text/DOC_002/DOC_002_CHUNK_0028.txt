23. 	Treatment with glucose-lowering medication among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Treatment with glucose-lowering medication among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess the level of treatment with glucose-lowering medication among people 
with diabetes. 
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who required glucose-lowering medication 
and were receiving it according to WHO or national guidelines:
	
●
This indicator can be derived from population-level surveys or health 
facility-based reporting systems. “Receiving medication” will be assessed 
through self-reports in a population-based survey, while in health facility-
based reporting system, it will be assessed through prescription data.
Numerator
Population survey data: number of respondents with diabetes who required 
glucose-lowering medication and were receiving it according to guidelines.
Facility data: number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities 
who required glucose-lowering medication and were receiving it according to 
guidelines during the reporting period.
Denominator
Population survey data: total number of respondents with diabetes who required 
glucose-lowering medication according to guidelines.
Facility data: total number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities 
who required glucose-lowering medication according to guidelines during the 
reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records, population-based survey
Limitations/comments
None

24. 	Insulin treatment among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Insulin treatment among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess the level of insulin treatment among people with diabetes. 
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who required insulin treatment and were 
receiving it according to WHO or national guidelines.
	
●
This indicator can be derived from population-level surveys or health 
facility-based reporting systems. “Receiving treatment” will be assessed 
through self-reports in a population-based survey, while in health facility-
based reporting system, it will be assessed through prescription data.
Numerator
Population survey data: number of respondents with diabetes who required 
insulin treatment and were receiving it according to guidelines.
Facility data: number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who 
required insulin treatment and were receiving it according to guidelines during 
the reporting period.
Denominator
Population survey data: total number of respondents with diabetes who required 
insulin treatment according to guidelines.
Facility data: total number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities 
who required insulin treatment according to guidelines.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, type of diabetes and type of insulin, stratify 
by facility level of care type, provider ownership type (public/private), and 
individual-level characteristics such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity 
status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence type (urban/rural), and 
health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records, population-based survey
Limitations/comments
None
