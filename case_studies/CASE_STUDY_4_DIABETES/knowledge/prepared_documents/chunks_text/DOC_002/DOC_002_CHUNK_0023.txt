13.	 Overweight and obesity assessment 
Indicator name
Overweight and obesity assessment
Purpose
To assess the level of overweight and obesity assessment in health facilities.
Definition
Proportion of people who were attending health facilities and assessed for 
overweight and obesity using height, weight and/or body mass index (BMI) 
measurements.
Numerator
Number of people who were attending health facilities and were assessed for 
overweight and obesity using height, weight and/or BMI measurements during 
the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people who attended health facilities in the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by diabetes status, obesity or overweight 
status and individual-level characteristics such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, 
comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence 
type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
For any comparison over time or with other populations, age 
standardization is needed.
Related links

noncommunicable-diseases/surveillance/systems-tools/steps, accessed 
26 May 2024).

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
14.	 Tobacco use assessment
Indicator name
Tobacco use assessment
Purpose
To assess the level of tobacco use assessment 
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who were attending health facilities and 
were assessed for tobacco use by a health professional in the facility during the 
reporting period.
Tobacco products include cigarettes, pipes, cigars, cigarillos, waterpipes (hookah, 
shisha), bidis, kretek, heated tobacco products, and all forms of smokeless (oral 
and nasal) tobacco. Tobacco products exclude products that do not contain 
tobacco, such as electronic nicotine delivery system (ENDS) of which e-cigarettes 
are a common type, as well as ‘e-cigars’, ‘e-hookahs’, JUUL and ‘e-pipes’.
Currently using means either daily or non-daily (occasional) use at the 
time of visit.
The assessment of tobacco use, defined as a series of questions on intensity, 
prolongation, type of product and method of consumption, in the current and 
in the past.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes who were attending health facilities and were 
assessed for tobacco use during the reporting period
Denominator
Number of people with diabetes who attended health facilities during the 
reporting period
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, facility level of care type, provider ownership 
type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, sex, race/
ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence 
type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
