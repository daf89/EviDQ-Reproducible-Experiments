25. 	Statin treatment among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Statin treatment among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess compliance with WHO or national guidelines regarding the initiation 
and continuation of statin treatment among individuals diagnosed with diabetes.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes aged 40 years and over who required 
statin treatment and were receiving it according to WHO or national 
treatment guidelines.
	
●
This indicator can be derived from population-level surveys or health 
facility-based reporting systems. “Receiving treatment” will be assessed 
through self-reports in a population-based survey, while in health facility-
based reporting system, it will be assessed through prescription data.
Numerator
Population survey data: number of respondents with diabetes aged 40 years and 
over who required statin treatment and were receiving it according to guidelines.
Facility data: number of people with diabetes aged 40 years and over registered 
in health facilities who required statin treatment and were receiving it according 
to guidelines during the reporting period.
Denominator
Population survey data: total number of respondents with diabetes aged 40 years 
and over who required statin treatment according to guidelines.
Facility data: total number of people with diabetes aged 40 years and over 
registered in health facilities who required statin treatment according 
to guidelines.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records, population-based survey
Limitations/comments
None

26. 	Treatment with blood pressure-lowering medication among people with
