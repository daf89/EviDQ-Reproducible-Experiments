11. 	Availability of glycated haemoglobin testing 
Indicator name
Availability of glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) testing
Purpose
To assess the availability of testing services for diabetes diagnosis 
and management.
Definition
Proportion of health facilities that are providing diabetes service and 
performing HbA1c test.
Numerator
Number of health facilities that are providing diabetes service and performing 
HbA1c test during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of health facilities
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Facility level of care type, provider ownership type (public/private), facility 
location type (urban/rural), HbA1c testing site (laboratory-based or point-of-
care testing)
Sources of data
Health facility reports, logistics information system or survey
Limitations/comments
In some settings, the health facilities do not provide laboratory services, so the 
reporting units will need to come from other laboratory service providers, or 
health facilities only collect samples on site and perform the test off site.
Related links

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
12. 	Availability and quality of diabetes surveillance system
Indicator name
Availability and quality of a diabetes surveillance system 
Purpose
To assess the availability of quality and comprehensive surveillance data for 
diabetes programme management.
Definition
Country has an established diabetes surveillance system comprising of all 
essential components if:
	
●
Country conducts a standardized population-based health survey that 
includes availability and quality of diabetes services every 3 to 5 years.
	
●
Country has an electronic, standardized system for recording person- and 
facility-level data that includes diabetes, with high level of coverage.
	
●
Country has a type 1 diabetes registry with national level coverage.
	
●
Country conducts regular standardized health facility surveys on 
availability of health facility services including diabetes management 
services and on capacities of health facilities to provide these services at 
required standards of quality, every 1 to 3 years.
	
●
Country has a well-functioning CRVS system for generating reliable 
cause-specific mortality data on a routine basis. The International Form 
of Medical Certificate of the Cause of Death is completed by certifiers. 
The International Classification of Diseases (ICD) is used to code the 
causes of death. Cause-of-death data are accessible to policy-makers and 
researchers. 
Numerator
Not applicable
Denominator
Not applicable
Method of estimation
1.	 Country can respond “Yes” to the following questions:
	
●
“Does your country conduct a population-based survey that includes 
diabetes?”. The country indicates frequency of conducting the population-
based survey (the country is considered to have available a regular 
standardized population-based survey that includes diabetes if there are 
published survey statistics at least every 3 to 5 years).
	
●
“Does your country have an electronic standardized system for 
recording person-and facility-level data that includes diabetes in public 
facilities?”(The country is considered to have an electronic standardized 
system if it has an electronic type of system that covers more than 50% of 
facilities).
	
●
“Does your country have a type 1 diabetes registry?”, indicate 
coverage (national, sub-national or others) and type (population- or 
hospital-based).
	
●
“Does your country conduct standardized surveys of facilities to assess 
availability of health facility services including diabetes management 
services and on capacities of health facilities to provide these services 
at required standards of quality?” – indicate the year of last facility 
survey (The country has available regular standardized health facility 
surveys, if there are published survey statistics on availability, readiness, 
quality, management, and financing of diabetes management services, 
every 1 to 3 years).
Country needs to provide a copy of documents to support their response to 
abovementioned questions.
