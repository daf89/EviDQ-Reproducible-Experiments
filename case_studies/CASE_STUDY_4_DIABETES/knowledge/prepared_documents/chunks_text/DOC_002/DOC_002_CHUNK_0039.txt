41. 	Lower-extremity amputation among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Lower-extremity amputation among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess outcome of diabetes management.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who had a lower-extremity amputation.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in the facility who had a lower- 
extremity amputation due to diabetic foot during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in the facility.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as 
age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic 
status, residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type, type of lower-
extremity amputation.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
For comparison with other health care facilities the indicator needs to be 
age-standardized.

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
42. 	Cardiovascular disease mortality rate
Indicator name
Cardiovascular disease mortality rate
Purpose
To measure impact of diabetes programme.
Definition
Number of cardiovascular disease deaths per 100 000 population.
Numerator
Number of cardiovascular disease deaths in a given period.
Denominator
Total population.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100 000
Countries using ICD-10 can include the codes: I00-I99 in the calculation of 
cardiovascular disease mortality.
Countries using ICD-11 can include the codes: 8B00-8B2Z, BA00-BE2Z in the 
calculation of cardiovascular disease mortality.  
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by type of CVD, individual characteristics 
such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, 
socioeconomic status, residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Civil registration with complete coverage and medical certification of 
cause of death.
Limitations/comments
For any comparison over time or with other populations, age 
standardisation is needed.
When calculating deaths by cause using ICD-10, WHO reassigns deaths 
assigned to ill-defined causes of death, such as those assigned to signs and 
symptoms (R00-R94, R96- R99), heart failure (I50), unspecified cardiac arrest (I46), 
and unspecified respiratory failure (J96). 
Related links
ICD-11 for mortality and morbidity statistics. Geneva: World Health Organization; 
2024 (https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en, accessed 26 May 2024).
WHO methods and data sources for country-level causes of death. Geneva: World 
Health Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/374896).
