15.	 Cardiovascular disease risk assessment 
Indicator name
Cardiovascular disease risk assessment 
Purpose
To assess the level of cardiovascular disease risk assessment among people aged 
40 years and over in health facilities.
Definition
Proportion of people aged 40 years and over who were attending health facilities 
and assessed for cardiovascular disease risk using WHO CVD risk charts.
Numerator
Number of people aged 40 years and over who were attending health facilities 
and were assessed for cardiovascular disease risk using WHO CVD risk charts 
during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people aged 40 years and over who attended health facilities 
during the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by diabetes status within each facility, 
facility level of care type, provider ownership type (public/private), and 
individual-level characteristics such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity 
status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence type (urban/rural), and 
health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records 
Limitations/comments
None

16.	 Hypertension screening 
Indicator name
Hypertension screening 
Purpose
To determine the level of opportunistic screening for hypertension.
Definition
Proportion of people aged 18 years and over who were attending health facilities 
and were screened for hypertension based on WHO or national guidelines.
Numerator
Number of people aged 18 years and over who were attending health facilities 
and were screened for hypertension during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people aged 18 years and over who attended health facilities 
during the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
Related links
Guideline for the pharmacological treatment of hypertension in adults. Geneva: 
World Health Organization; 2021 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/344424).

indicators and application. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2022 (https://iris.
