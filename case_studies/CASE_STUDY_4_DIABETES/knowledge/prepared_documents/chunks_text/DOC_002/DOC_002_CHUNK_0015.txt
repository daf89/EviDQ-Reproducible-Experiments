Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
1. 	 Existence and implementation of national diabetes prevention and 
control plan 
Indicator name
Existence and implementation of national diabetes prevention and 
control plan 
Purpose
To assess the availability of governance mechanisms for diabetes prevention 
and control at national level.
Definition
Country has a national diabetes prevention and control plan developed/
updated within the past 5 years and the plan is currently implemented.
	
●
A national diabetes prevention and control plan includes a set of specific 
strategic actions to be taken to achieve the goals of the national diabetes 
prevention and control programme. The plan may be an independent 
diabetes prevention and control plan or part of a comprehensive national 
plan for noncommunicable diseases. 
Numerator
Not applicable
Denominator
Not applicable 
Method of estimation 
Country can respond “Yes” to the question “Does the country have a national 
plan that includes prevention, early detection, treatment and/or care for 
diabetes?” and needs to provide a copy of the plan and the latest report on the 
implementation of the plan.
Existence of the plan is validated by examining the plan document and 
implementation is validated by examining the latest report detailing the 
execution of at least one strategic action outlined in the plan.
The indicator is considered “Yes” if the country has a national diabetes 
prevention and control plan developed/updated within the past 5 years and the 
plan is currently being implemented.
The indicator is considered “Partially yes” if the country has a national diabetes 
prevention and control plan developed/updated within the past 5 years, but it is 
not currently being implemented.
The indicator is considered “No” if the country does not have a national diabetes 
prevention and control plan developed/updated within the past 5 years.
Disaggregation
Type of diabetes included in the plan
Sources of data
NCD country capacity survey, other survey, country report 
Limitations/comments
An acceptable minimum number of strategic actions completed according to 
plan must be defined at every evaluation period to consider the plan as currently 
implemented.
Related links

ncdccs, accessed 26 May 2024).
