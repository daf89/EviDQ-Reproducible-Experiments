27. 	Retinopathy treatment among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Retinopathy treatment among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess the level of retinopathy treatment among people with diabetes in 
health facilities.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes and retinopathy who received retinopathy 
treatment based on guidelines.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes and retinopathy registered in health facilities 
who received retinopathy treatment according to guidelines during the 
reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes and retinopathy registered in health 
facilities during the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
Related links
HEARTS-D: diagnosis and management of type 2 diabetes. Geneva: World Health 
Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/331710).

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
28. 	Diabetic foot treatment 
Indicator name
Diabetic foot treatment 
Purpose
To assess the level of diabetic foot treatment in health facilities.
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes who developed diabetic foot and received 
clinical treatment for diabetic foot according to WHO or national guidelines.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who developed 
diabetic foot and received clinical treatment for diabetic foot according to 
guidelines during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who 
developed diabetic foot during the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual-level characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
Related links
HEARTS-D: diagnosis and management of type 2 diabetes.  Geneva: World 
Health Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/331710). WHO 
package of essential noncommunicable (PEN) disease interventions for primary 
health care. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/
handle/10665/334186).
