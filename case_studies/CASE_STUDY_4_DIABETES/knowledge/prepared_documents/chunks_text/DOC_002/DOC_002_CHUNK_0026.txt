19. 	Blood glucose measurement among people with diabetes 
Indicator name
Fasting plasma glucose measurement among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess the level of blood glucose measurement among people with diabetes 
in health facilities. 
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who were tested 
for glycaemic control using glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) or fasting plasma 
glucose (FPG) measurement.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who were tested for 
glycaemic control using HbA1c or FPG measurement during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Type of measurement (HbA1c or FPG) and where possible and applicable, 
stratify by facility level of care type, provider ownership type (public/private), and 
individual characteristics such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, 
high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, residence type (urban/rural), and health 
insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
Related links
HEARTS-D: diagnosis and management of type 2 diabetes. Geneva: World Health 
Organization; 2020 (https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/331710).

Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
20. 	Retinopathy assessment among people with diabetes
Indicator name
Retinopathy assessment among people with diabetes
Purpose
To assess the level of retinopathy assessment among people with diabetes in 
health facilities. 
Definition
Proportion of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who were 
due for their screening for diabetic retinopathy and who received the 
screening involving: 
	
●
visual acuity; and
	
●
direct/indirect ophthalmoscopy (dilated pupils) or retinal fundus 
photography.
The frequency of screening can be defined in accordance with WHO (biennial 
screening) or national guidelines.
Numerator
Number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who were due for 
their biennial screening for diabetic retinopathy and who received the screening 
during the reporting period.
Denominator
Total number of people with diabetes registered in health facilities who were due 
for their biennial screening for diabetic retinopathy during the reporting period.
Method of estimation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by facility level of care type, provider 
ownership type (public/private), and individual characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Health facility patient registers, health records
Limitations/comments
None
