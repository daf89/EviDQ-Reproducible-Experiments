Guidance on global monitoring for diabetes prevention and control: framework, indicators and application
29. 	Physical inactivity prevalence 
Indicator name
Physical inactivity prevalence
Purpose
To measure the prevalence of physical inactivity among people aged 18 
years and over.
Definition
Proportion of people aged 18 years and over who are insufficiently physically 
active (i.e. not meeting WHO recommendation on physical activity for health, 
which is equivalent to at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity 
per week or equivalent).
Throughout a week, including activity for work, during transport and leisure time, 
adults should do at least: 
	
●
150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity; or
	
●
75 minutes of vigorous-intensity physical activity; or
	
●
an equivalent combination of moderate- and vigorous-intensity physical 
activity achieving at least 600 metabolic equivalents (MET)-minutes.
Physical inactivity can be measured using the Global physical activity 
questionnaire. 
Numerator
Number of respondents aged 18 years and over, who were insufficiently 
physically active.
Denominator
Total number of respondents aged 18 years and over.
Method of calculation
Numerator ÷ denominator × 100
Disaggregation
Where possible and applicable, stratify by individual characteristics such as age, 
sex, race/ethnicity, comorbidity status, high-risk groups, socioeconomic status, 
residence type (urban/rural), and health insurance type.
Sources of data
Nationally or sub-nationally representative population-based surveys with data 
on physical activity using standard measurement tool.
Limitations/comments
For any comparison over time or with other populations, age 
standardization is needed.
