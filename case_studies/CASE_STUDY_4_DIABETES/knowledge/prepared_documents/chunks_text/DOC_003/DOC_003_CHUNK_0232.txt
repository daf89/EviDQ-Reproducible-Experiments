salt per day 
Levels of sodium and creatinine in spot urine samples are used in STEPS to 
estimate population 24 hour salt intake, using the INTERSALT equation: 
 
Estimated 24 hour sodium (Na) intake in mmol for males: 23.51+0.45*spot Na 
concentration (mmol/L) -3.09*spot creatinine concentration 
(mmol/L)+4.16*BMI+0.22*Age 
 
Estimated 24 hour sodium (Na) intake in mmol for females: 3.74+0.33* spot Na 
concentration (mmol/L)-2.44* spot creatinine concentration (mmol/L)+2.42* BMI 
+2.34* Age -0.03* Age ^2 
 
The 24 hour sodium values in mmol are divided by 17.1 in order to get grams of salt. 
 
 
 
WHO 
recommen- 
dation 
The WHO recommendation is less than 5 grams of salt or 2 grams of sodium per 
person per day.
