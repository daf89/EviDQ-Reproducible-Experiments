Physical Measurements Overview 
 
 
 
Introduction 
Step 2 of the STEPS Instrument includes selected physical measures to 
determine the proportion of adults that: 
 
• 
have raised blood pressure 
• 
are overweight and/or obese. 
 
 
What you will 
learn 
In this section, you will learn: 
 
• what the physical measures are and what they mean 
• what equipment is needed 
• how to assemble and use the equipment 
• how to take physical measurements and accurately record the results. 
 
 
 
Learning 
outcomes 
objectives 
The learning outcome of this section is to understand what the physical 
measures are and how to accurately take the measurements and record the 
results.
