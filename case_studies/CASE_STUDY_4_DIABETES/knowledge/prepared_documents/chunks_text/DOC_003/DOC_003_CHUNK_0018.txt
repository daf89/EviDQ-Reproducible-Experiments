Overview 
 
 
Introduction 
There are a number of entities involved in a STEPS survey at different levels 
including: 
 
• country 
• regional 
• global. 
 
They all have key roles, which are described below. 
 
 
Purpose 
The purpose of this section is to: 
 
• provide an overview of the relationships between all those involved in a 
STEPS survey; 
• provide a description of each of the core roles involved.
