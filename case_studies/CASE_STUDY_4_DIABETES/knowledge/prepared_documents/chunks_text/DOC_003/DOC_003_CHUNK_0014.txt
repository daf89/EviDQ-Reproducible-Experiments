STEPS 
Instrument 
The STEPS tool used to collect data and measure NCD risk factors is called 
the STEPS Instrument. 
 
The STEPS Instrument covers three different levels, or 'Steps', of risk factor 
assessment: Step 1, Step 2 and Step 3, as follows: 
 
 
 
Step 
Description 
Purpose 
Recommendation

To measure prevalence of high

raised blood glucose, and
